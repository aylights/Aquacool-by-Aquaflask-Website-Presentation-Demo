Campaign notes and rationale
Execution

Digital marketing: an interactive product site

The site has three tabs. Design your jacket is the main feature: riders pick Aquaflask colors, find their size, choose a bundle and buy. About Aquacool tells the brand story: how the cooling layers work, the heat comparison, and rider word of mouth. Events & Stores covers Ride and Try at moto conventions (February and May 2027), mall pop-up booths during the March–April hot season, and where to buy all year.

Message

"We keep your drinks cool. Now it's your turn."

Aquaflask already owns "cold" in people's minds. Aquacool stretches that promise from the bottle to the rider: stay cool, stay fresh.

Target market

Gen X and Millennial urban riders

Riders who need a jacket for sun and road protection but hate how hot and sticky regular riding jackets get in Philippine traffic.

Rationale: the page follows AIDA

Each section moves the rider one step closer to buying

  • Attention (hero): the familiar Aquaflask "cold" promise and the tagline, with the rider's own jacket on screen.
  • Interest (product): the four-layer cross-section, feature cards and the heat slider explain the technology and prove the benefit visually.
  • Desire (design): riders build a jacket in Aquaflask colors. The quiz, classic dark builds and size guide make it easy for every age group, and people value what they help create.
  • Action (deals): bundle tiers with visible savings, a live order summary and a Buy now button that follows the rider down the page.
  • Distribution (Place): pre-orders on the Aquacool website (the only channel for custom colors), the official Aquaflask website and its official Shopee and Lazada stores, and selected Aquaflask stores at launch, then partner motorcycle gear shops. Online reaches riders where they research and buy on payday; Aquaflask stores use existing foot traffic and the bottle bundle; moto shops put Aquacool next to Duhan and Komine.
  • Event marketing: Ride and Try ("Feel the difference. Beat the heat."): booths at motorcycle conventions with a try-on area, product comparison, demo area, Cooling Challenge and supervised test rides, turning an unfamiliar benefit into something riders feel. The website supports it with test-ride registration and a word-of-mouth section for vlogger and rider feedback. KPIs: trial registrations, earned media reach and mentions, early-access sign-ups, and cooling satisfaction ratings.
  • Trial (pop-up booths): booths at six Metro Manila malls offer try-ons and a live heat test, removing the last doubt about buying riding gear online.
  • Material choice: PU leather keeps Aquacool at an accessible price, is lighter and easier to clean than cowhide, and holds bright Aquaflask colors well, matching the parent brand's "affordable quality" position.
  • Pricing: ₱2,999 for every jacket, ready build or custom colors. Charm pricing keeps it under the ₱3,000 mark, and charging the same for custom colors makes the main feature feel like a free upgrade. Bundles pair the jacket with Aquaflask: the Cooling Duo (jacket + matching bottle, ₱3,799) links the new product to the one riders already own, and the Cool Season Bundle adds a spare pair of gel packs (₱4,199).
  • Accessibility: the English/Filipino toggle and Night view (showing the reflective trim) speak to the rider's real language and commute.
  • Word of mouth: the downloadable build card and share caption with #StayCoolStayFresh turn customers into promoters in rider groups.
Where it fits in the customer journey
  1. Awareness: "Beyond the Bottle" teaser ads point to this site.
  2. Introduction: moto-vloggers review the jacket after Ride and Try test rides, and riders share their build cards.
  3. Trial: Ride and Try booths at motorcycle conventions (try-on, Cooling Challenge, supervised test ride) plus mall pop-up booths.
  4. Conversion: pre-orders and jacket + Aquaflask bundles through the Buy now flow.
  5. Retention: replacement gel packs offered in the colors of the rider's build.
